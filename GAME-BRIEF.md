# AIREGIN | Nadabi at dusk

Replace the decision dashboard at /play/ with an embodied third-person 3D browser game. The existing marketing page remains intact.

Player goal: restore power before dusk, uncover an optional council diversion, choose whose interests to protect, and make the announcement at the public square.

Loop: navigate the neighbourhood → speak to people / collect a fuse → repair the substation → investigate or make a compromised allocation → announce the outcome → replay or freely explore.

Visual target: warm, authored, stylised Nigerian streets. Corrugated roofs, concrete compounds, market awnings, yellow minibuses, overhead wires, water tanks, palms, warm evening light. One coherent procedural art set; no generated images.

Controls: keyboard movement, sprint, jump, proximity interaction, mouse/touch orbit, dual touch controls. Collision, camera occlusion, stamina, moving pedestrians, sound toggle, pause, local save and full retry loop.

Scope: playable solo district with scripted characters and branching consequences. No claims of online multiplayer or production scale.

Acceptance: title → controllable world → successful physical objective loop → distinct endings → replay; collision and camera handle buildings; inputs reset on focus loss; pause freezes simulation; unavailable WebGL reports a useful error; touch controls respect safe areas. Desktop browser verification and pure logic checks. Real phone performance remains unverified.

Skills: game router, threejs-scene-setup, threejs-materials-lighting, game-feel, input-systems, camera-systems, game-ui-ux, level-design, audio-design, mobile-native, Emil design engineering.

Runtime: local Three.js r180, MIT. Adapted to r180 APIs, not the r186 skill baseline.
